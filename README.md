# The Harley Street Eye Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The Harley Street Eye Clinic is a specialist ophthalmology clinic based in Marylebone, London, providing consultant-led eye care and treatment for a wide range of eye conditions. Its services include cataract surgery, retinal care, glaucoma treatment, macular degeneration and specialist ophthalmology consultations.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic's specialist expertise, experienced consultants and patient-centred approach through a clearer, more professional healthcare website experience. The design aims to help prospective patients understand the clinic's services, establish confidence and access appointment enquiries more easily.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Specialist Positioning
- Improved the presentation of the clinic's ophthalmology services and specialist treatment areas.
- Made the clinic's core expertise clearer and more prominent.
- Created a more focused introduction to help visitors quickly understand the clinic's purpose and specialisations.

### 2. Greater Consultant Visibility
- Increased the emphasis on consultant expertise and clinical specialisations.
- Improved the presentation of the clinic's medical professionals to make their experience and areas of practice easier to identify.
- Strengthened the trust-building aspect of the homepage by giving clinical expertise greater prominence.

### 3. Clearer Appointment Journey
- Improved the visibility and placement of appointment and enquiry calls to action.
- Made contact options easier to identify throughout the homepage.
- Focused on creating a more straightforward journey for visitors looking to arrange a consultation or contact the clinic.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more polished, professional healthcare aesthetic appropriate for a specialist London eye clinic.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Improved the organisation of homepage content so specialist services, consultant information and appointment options are easier to navigate.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer browsing experience with more accessible information and prominent next-step actions.
- **Content Consistency:** Improved the presentation of clinical services and consultant information to create a more coherent homepage.
- **Overall Layout:** Structured the homepage around the clinic's expertise, treatment offerings and patient enquiries, making the information easier to understand and access.

---

**Project Focus:** Specialist positioning, consultant credibility and a more accessible appointment journey.
